# SECTION C — Where CID wins (show in demo)

1. **Segment brand** — Customer arrives as a **plumber / salon / trainer**, not as a generic “small business” comparison shopper.
2. **CONNECT — Instant COI hub** — GC needs proof on site; saved holders; reissue in one tap (~60-second story).
3. **Am I Covered?** — Mid-job question answered from **their actual policy**, trade-aware plain language — not a generic FAQ.
4. **Renewal / switching cost** — Certificate holder book, timeline, and habit in-app before renewal shopping starts.
5. **Multi-line, one relationship** — BOP/GL + Workers’ Comp (and future lines) tied to **one submission / one app experience** as rails mature.

**Moat line (investor / strategic):**  
*A contractor with 18 GC certificate holders saved in CONNECT does not leave at renewal. Rebuilding that book elsewhere costs more than staying.*

# SECTION F — Strategic frame for carriers and MGAs

**Carrier meetings:** lead with **`CID_BATTLE_CARD_strategic_acquirer_platform.md`** (Acquire the customer platform · attach appetite · demo second). This section supports marketplace context and Simply fact sheet.

### The choice many carriers face

| Path | What happens |
|------|----------------|
| **A — Ignore digital micro-SMB** | Premium and customer relationship flow to **national digital brokers** (Travelers / Simply Business–class) and comparison funnels. Legacy agency cost structure remains; **growth in sub-$5k** goes elsewhere. |
| **B — Build in-house** | Typical carrier IT: **18–24+ months**, tens of millions, core-system priority. Often delivers **quote** but rarely a **daily-use post-bind product** for micro accounts. |
| **C — Partner with or acquire CID** | **Live** ConnectQuote + CONNECT + segment replication; attach **your** appetite and rails; acquire and service sub-$5k **profitably** without funding a competitor’s marketplace. |

### Talk track (carrier executive)

*“Travelers already bought its position in digital micro-commercial. The question is what everyone else is going to do about it — how many years and how much capital [Carrier X] will spend building trade acquisition, bind, and post-bind in one stack — versus attaching to a platform that is live today: **ACQUIRE** by trade, **TRANSACT** on ConnectQuote, **OWN THE RELATIONSHIP** in CONNECT.”*

**Kicker:** *Attach your appetite to the platform without rebuilding the platform.*

### Talk track (investor)

*Simply Business **validates** micro-SMB digital demand. CID is **different product architecture** — segment brands plus post-bind platform — with **strategic value to any carrier that does not want Travelers to own the entire digital long tail.*

### Economics reminder (carrier)

- Internal labor **$250–500+** per policy file touch
- Typical **~$1,500** BOP → **~$180** commission at ~12%
- **Servicing** certificates and calls on sub-$5k accounts is structurally loss-making for traditional models
- CID thesis: **CONNECT** drives marginal post-bind service cost toward **~$0** while commission **compounds on renewal**

# SECTION H — Deep reference: Simply Business (fact sheet)

**Use for diligence or appendix; trim for live pitch.**

### Ownership and corporate

- **Parent:** The Travelers Companies, Inc. (NYSE: TRV)
- **Acquisition:** Announced March 2017; closed August 2017; ~**$490M** enterprise value
- **Seller:** Aquiline Capital Partners
- **Travelers reporting:** Simply Business, LLC listed as subsidiary; included in **Business Insurance** segment (with UK operations and other units)
- **Operating model stated at close:** Continue as **standalone** brand; partner with **panel of carriers**

### Business model

- **Licensed insurance producer / digital broker** — not the underwriting carrier
- Customer completes **one application**; platform returns **multiple quotes** from partner insurers
- Customer selects; **carrier issues policy**, bills, handles claims; broker earns **commission**
- Revenue: placement commission; carrier programs at scale

### U.S. customer journey (marketing-aligned)

1. Enter business details on **quote.simplybusiness.com** (or main site funnel)
2. Compare options from **panel** of carriers
3. Purchase online; for many products, **COI** available quickly via **online account**
4. Ongoing: account for COI, documents, billing; **phone agents** for support; some policy changes require **carrier or contact center**

### Post-bind capabilities (public product)

- Online account: COI download, add **certificate holders** / additional insureds, email share
- **Apple Wallet / QR** for COI (per help center)
- Policy documents and payment method updates
- Claims: directed to **issuing carrier** (Simply Business does not pay claims)

### Acquisition / SEO

- **Single master brand:** simplybusiness.com
- Trade **content/landing pages:** e.g. plumber insurance, contractor insurance — **URL paths**, not independent trade domains
- Messaging emphasis: **speed**, **compare**, **affordability**, “overpaying”

### Third-party sentiment (pattern)

- **Positive:** fast quoting, easy online purchase, helpful agents for some
- **Mixed:** post-purchase confusion when **billing/documents** come from unfamiliar **carrier** name; **claims experience** depends on issuer; servicing not always consistent

### Overlap note (internal)

Industry sources list **multiple** instant-bind and specialty carriers on marketplace panels — **the same commercial lines partners CID uses may appear on broker panels**. CID must win on **full journey, segment brand, CONNECT retention**, not exclusive product access.
