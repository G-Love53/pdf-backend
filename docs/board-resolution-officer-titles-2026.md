## Resolution 2 — Roles and responsibilities

### Raymond Schiavone — Executive Chairman

Capital strategy and fundraising; investor and SAFE investor relationships; exit architecture and M&A strategy; major strategic partnerships; Board leadership and governance; financial strategy; corporate development. **Primary executive for strategic transactions** including any sale or acquisition of the Corporation.

### Gerry Jones — Chief Executive Officer

Day-to-day operations and execution; platform architecture and technology strategy; ConnectQuote, Connect, and RSS Engine; segment launches and go-to-market execution; carrier API integrations; customer acquisition operations; platform marketing. **Primary executive responsible for running the business.**

### Richard Cline — Chief Revenue Officer

Insurance licensing and regulatory compliance; carrier relationships and appointments; distribution strategy; **producer of record for all policy transactions**; Coterie MGA appointment; GUARD Insurance Workers’ Comp program development; revenue relationships. **Primary executive responsible for insurance infrastructure and revenue generation.**
