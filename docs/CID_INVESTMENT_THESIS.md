## The One-Sentence Version

Commercial Insurance Direct is not an insurance carrier or an MGA — it is the **commercial insurance customer platform** for America's most underserved market: the $40 billion sub-$5,000 micro-commercial segment that traditional agencies and carriers cannot profitably serve.

---

## The Problem

The economics of servicing small commercial insurance have broken. This is structural, not cyclical.

A carrier or agency spends **$250–$500 in internal labor to touch a single policy file**. On a $1,500 BOP generating $180 in commission, that is a structural loss before a single claim is paid. The result: roughly half the insurance workforce is retiring this decade with no digital-native successor, and **$40 billion in sub-$5,000 commercial premium is being systematically abandoned** by the carriers and agencies that used to write it.

Carriers want the premium. They cannot afford the people to write it.

## The CID Platform — Three Integrated Layers

CID acquires, qualifies, distributes, and services small commercial insurance customers through a technology platform purpose-built for underserved low-premium businesses. No underwriting risk. No carrier paper. Asset-light and carrier-agnostic by design.

### 1 · Acquire — Segment Storefronts
Ten-plus trade-specific websites, each purpose-built for a single type of small business. Segment-native language, intake forms, and coverage explanations that convert where generalist portals fail. New segments deploy on shared infrastructure in **under 5 hours** from domain purchase to live ConnectQuote intake.

### 2 · Distribute — ConnectQuote + RSS Engine
CID's instant quote-and-bind platform powered by the Coterie API. A small business owner completes a trade-specific intake form and receives a real-time BOP/GL quote. They bind directly. The policy is issued immediately. No human touchpoints. The RSS Engine provides a dual rail: Coterie API instant-bind for supported carriers, automated ACORD S1–S6 for every other carrier regardless of technology maturity. GUARD Insurance (W.R. Berkley) Workers' Comp — sandbox E2E tested on ConnectQuote (Aug 2026); prod gated on carrier credentials — second commercial line on the platform.

### 3 · Retain — CONNECT
CONNECT is a branded mobile app — always available, policy-specific, always with the insured on phone, tablet, or desktop — that transforms insurance from a one-time transaction into a living, working relationship. Policy vault, instant COI hub, Am I Covered?, claims initiation, and renewal management. The bind is the beginning of the relationship, not the end. Traditional agencies retain at 80–85% because clients shop at renewal. CONNECT is designed to break that pattern permanently by raising switching costs before renewal arrives.

> A roofer with 18 GC certificate holders saved in CONNECT doesn't leave at renewal. Rebuilding that book elsewhere is more expensive than staying. That is the moat.

## Why Now — Six Converging Forces

1. **Aging independent agency workforce** — half the industry retires this decade with no digital-native successor
2. **$40B underserved segment** — structural carrier abandonment creates an open infrastructure gap
3. **Carrier appetite for digital distribution** — Allianz X invested in Coterie specifically for SMB; GUARD building Workers' Comp for ConnectQuote
4. **API-first underwriting now commercially available** — instant bind was uneconomical to build five years ago; it is commercially available today through Coterie
5. **Small business owners expect digital experiences** — they manage banking, payroll, and accounting on their phones; insurance is the last holdout
6. **Technology compressing servicing costs to near zero** — AI and automation drive marginal cost of post-bind service toward zero via CONNECT self-service

The fundamental advantage: adding 1,000 policyholders to a traditional agency requires proportional headcount growth. Adding 1,000 policyholders to CID requires near-zero incremental cost.

## The Exit Thesis

CID is valued as **tech-enabled distribution infrastructure**, not a traditional agency. The exit conversation is with a carrier or MGA that needs CID's infrastructure to profitably capture the $40B segment they are currently abandoning.

**A carrier with $50M+ in sub-$4K commercial GWP** spends $7–14M annually in internal service cost on that book. CID at a $15–20M acquisition price pays for itself in under two years on cost elimination alone — before counting the new premium the carrier can now profitably write through ConnectQuote.

**The realistic acquirer universe includes 30–43 carriers, MGAs, and wholesale brokers** — including GUARD (existing relationship), Coterie/Allianz X (API partner), Hiscox and Intact (Coterie investors), Amwins (prior white-label relationship with the founding team), and BrokerTech Ventures founding partners (Cincinnati Insurance, EMC Insurance, AF Group).

**Target exit: $15–25M strategic acquisition within 18–24 months** of SAFE close, driven by platform data — proven CAC, CONNECT retention through first renewal cycle, and multi-carrier architecture demonstrated.
