# SECTION A — Executive summary (all audiences)

### Who “they” are (30 seconds)

| | |
|--|--|
| **Brand** | **ERGO NEXT Insurance** (rebrand from NEXT Insurance; “NEXT Connect” is the **embedded** product) |
| **Category** | **Digital commercial carrier / agency** with **embedded distribution** (B2B2C) — not a neutral broker marketplace |
| **Ownership** | **Munich Re Group** — **100% acquisition** completed **July 2025**; ~**$2.6 billion** valuation; managed under **ERGO** (Munich Re primary insurance arm) |
| **Scale (public, 2024–2025)** | **750,000+** small-business customers cited; **~$548M** premium (2024); **1,300+** professions; **~700** employees; Palo Alto HQ |
| **Partner pitch (NEXT Connect)** | Embed quote-to-bind **inside your platform** (payroll, fintech, vertical SaaS); low/no-code; prefilled/instant quote; optional in-house licensed agency support |
| **Direct / agent paths** | Also sells **direct** on nextinsurance.com and via **digital agent** appointment (Agent Copilot) — separate from Connect |
| **Post-bind (their claim)** | 24/7 DIY: manage policies, **COI**, additional insured, claims online; “no extra fees” on COI (marketing) |
| **Underwriting story** | AI/ML underwriting; **30+ carriers** referenced for capacity behind the experience; A+ (Superior) financial strength narrative via Munich Re |

**Internal message:** ERGO NEXT is a **well-funded, carrier-backed digital incumbent** — closer to **“Stripe for SMB insurance embed”** than to Simply Business’s **compare-and-buy broker**. They are a **platform partner option** and a **competitive reference** for digital completeness, not a small InsurTech experiment.

### CID in one sentence (vs ERGO NEXT)

**CID owns trade-branded acquisition and a multi-carrier customer platform (ConnectQuote + CONNECT) — neutral to carriers — instead of embedding one carrier group’s journey inside someone else’s app or NEXT’s direct funnel.**

### Elevator (15 seconds)

*Munich Re paid $2.6B for ERGO NEXT because US micro-SMB digital works. NEXT Connect lets Fortune 500 platforms embed **their** insurance. CID builds **your** segment brands and CONNECT so the contractor’s relationship stays with us — and any carrier rail we attach — not inside a payroll vendor’s white-label widget.*

# SECTION C — Where CID wins (show in demo)

1. **Neutral platform** — Other carriers can partner with CID without feeding **Munich Re’s** owned digital channel.
2. **Segment-branded front door** — Not a generic embed or 1,300-class dropdown; **trade identity** at acquisition.
3. **CONNECT depth** — **Saved certificate holders**, **Am I Covered?** from **their** policy in **plain language** for the trade — not only DIY COI download.
4. **Multi-rail architecture** — Instant bind + traditional path; add lines/partners without re-embedding a new carrier widget.
5. **Sub-$5k economics story** — Built for micro-commercial service cost → ~$0 post-bind, not only for embed conversion KPIs.

**Moat line:**  
*Embed partners optimize **conversion inside their product**. CID optimizes **daily use after bind** — the certificate book the owner won’t rebuild elsewhere.*

# SECTION D — Where they win (do not argue here)

- **Scale and capital** — $2.6B exit to Munich Re; 750k+ customers; dedicated partnership engineering team
- **Embedded distribution** — If you **are** a payroll/fintech platform, NEXT Connect is **purpose-built** for you
- **End-to-end digital completeness** — Quote, bind, COI, claims in one **mature** consumer experience
- **Single-vendor simplicity** — One partnership vs building multi-carrier ops (for embed hosts)
- **Brand trust** — A+ narrative, Munich Re backing, massive review volume
- **Breadth** — 1,300 professions vs CID’s focused segment rollout

**Pivot line:**  
*We’re not trying to be NEXT Connect for payroll companies. We’re building the **contractor’s insurance home** on the open web — and a stack other carriers can ride.*

| Path | Outcome |
|------|---------|

| **B — Build in-house** | Long, expensive; rarely matches embed + COI completeness |
| **C — Partner CID** | Neutral **ConnectQuote + CONNECT + segment** deployment; **your** appetite on **our** UX |

**Talk track:**  
*Munich Re didn’t buy a feature — they bought US SMB digital. Carriers that aren’t Munich Re need a **neutral** customer platform that already binds and services — or they rent someone else’s channel.*

# SECTION H — Deep reference: ERGO NEXT (fact sheet)

### Ownership and corporate

- **Founded:** 2016, Palo Alto; >$1B VC prior to Munich Re control
- **March 2025:** Definitive agreement — Munich Re to acquire **100%** at **~$2.6B** valuation
- **July 2025:** Closing; NEXT embedded in **ERGO** management; reinsurance substantially via **Munich Re America**
- **Brand:** Public-facing shift to **ERGO NEXT Insurance**; product name **NEXT Connect** for embed partners

### NEXT Connect (partner page, paraphrased)

- Embedded quote-to-bind **native to partner platform**
- Low/no-code integration; prefilled / instant quote
- Partner success team (sales, marketing, engineering, product, data)
- Claim: partners’ multi-product customers retain **~5% higher** (their stat)
- Target partners: “Unicorn” to **Fortune 500** platforms

### Products and servicing (marketing)

- BOP, GL, WC, and related commercial lines for SMB
- **1,300+** professions
- 24/7 online: policies, claims, proof of insurance, additional insured
- In-house licensed agency option alongside digital path

### Other channels (not NEXT Connect)

- **Direct:** nextinsurance.com
- **Agents:** Digital appointment / Agent Copilot (separate funnel from embed)

### CID internal note (strategy)

- NEXT **direct API** for custom stack has been **explored and deprioritized** relative to current **instant-bind partner rails** — treat NEXT Connect as **partnership/embed** path, not assumed ConnectQuote backend.
- Revisit only with clear **RSS** case: revenue, reliability, sellability vs owning UX.

# SECTION L — Internal only: partner, compete, or ignore?

| Option | When it fits | Risk |
|--------|----------------|------|
| **Partner (NEXT Connect)** | CID becomes **host platform** for insurance inside Connect or a segment SaaS partner; fast GL/BOP; Munich Re paper | **Customer + data** flow through ERGO NEXT; weakens multi-carrier neutral story |

**Decision lens (RSS):** Does embed **increase reliability** without locking one carrier? Does it **scale** beyond one partner IT queue? Is it **sellable** in an exit story?
