# CID Distribution Validation Plan

## Objective

Use the $250,000 SAFE to identify the first repeatable channel × vertical × state acquisition wedge that produces eligible customers, bound policies, attractive early economics, and scalable operational behavior.

CID is not using this capital to build the product. The product rails are live. The capital funds evidence needed to allocate future growth capital intelligently.

## Channel categories

- Vertical groups and trade associations
- Suppliers and distributors
- Trade software and embedded/workflow partners
- Compliant segmented direct outreach
- High-intent search
- Retargeting
- SEO and trade-specific content
- Carrier/MGA co-marketing or referral opportunities where appropriate

## Primary test dimensions

- Channel
- Vertical
- State
- Product mix: BOP/GL, workers' compensation, and appropriate additional lines
- Business profile: owner-only, first employee, small employer, and other relevant micro-business cohorts
- Carrier/product eligibility

## Core metrics

| Metric | Definition | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified visitor / prospect cost | Cost to reach a relevant business owner | Measures channel quality |
| Quote-start rate | Visitors/prospects who begin the flow | Measures message and landing-page relevance |
| Eligible quote rate | Quote starts that match product/carrier appetite | Measures targeting and product fit |
| Quote-to-bind rate | Eligible quoted prospects who bind | Measures flow, price, trust, and product fit |
| Cost per bound policy | Fully loaded channel spend divided by bound policies | Primary acquisition metric |
| Written premium | Premium produced per policy / cohort | Revenue and carrier relevance |
| WC attach | Percentage of eligible BOP/GL customers adding WC | Account expansion |
| CONNECT activation | Customers who activate and use CONNECT | Service scalability and retention signal |
| Service cost / exception rate | Human intervention after bind | Operational leverage |
| Retention / renewal | Cohort renewal behavior as data matures | Long-term economics |

## Capital-concentration rule

CID will concentrate capital behind cohorts that meet pre-set thresholds for eligible quote rate, bound-policy CAC, written premium, workers' compensation attach, CONNECT activation, and manageable exception/service cost.

## Current decision question

Which channel × vertical × state combination should receive the next increment of capital based on actual bind economics and operating behavior?

## Deliverables

- Weekly Growth Council brief
- Channel × vertical × state scorecard
- 10-state workers' compensation launch scorecard
- Partner pilot tracker
- Monthly SAFE deployment and learning memo
