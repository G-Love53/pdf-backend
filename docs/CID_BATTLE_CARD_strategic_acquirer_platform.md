# Change the psychology first

Do **not** open with:

*“Our startup is better than Simply Business.”*

Open with:

**“What is your digital micro-commercial strategy — and how many years and how much capital are you prepared to spend building it?”**

Then show something **already operating**.

**CID differentiation comes second.** Once they are thinking strategically, show why the platform is interesting.

# Market proof (one paragraph — not a comp claim)

**Travelers already bought its position in digital micro-commercial** (~$490M for Simply Business, 2017). That transaction does **not** mean CID is “the next $490M broker.” It means the capability was **strategically important enough to buy**.

**The question for everyone else:** What is your plan for trade-level acquisition, instant bind, and post-bind digital service — without rebuilding a national marketplace?

Use national digital brokers as **TAM and urgency**, not as the product CID is trying to clone.

# Strategic clock (build vs attach)

| Path | What happens |
|------|----------------|
| **A — Wait** | Micro-commercial digital and relationship flow to **consolidated digital brokers** (Travelers / marketplace-class) and price-led funnels. |
| **B — Build in-house** | Typical carrier IT: **18–24+ months**, tens of millions; often **quote** without a **daily-use post-bind product** for micro accounts. |
| **C — Partner with or acquire CID** | **Live** platform today: segment replication, ConnectQuote, CONNECT; add **your** programs and rails; compete for the long tail **without** funding a neutral competitor’s full rebuild. |

### Talk track (60 seconds)

*Travelers already bought its position in digital micro-commercial. The question is what everyone else is going to do about it.*

*We operate a **customer platform**: acquire by trade, bind on our stack, keep the owner in **CONNECT** for certificates and coverage every week on the job. You can **attach appetite** to that — partner or strategic acquisition — instead of spending years and nine figures to replicate acquisition plus bind plus post-bind in one product.*

# Economics reminder (sub-$5k)

- Internal labor often **$250–500+** per policy file touch  
- Typical **~$1,500** BOP → **~$180** commission at ~12%  
- Certificate and call volume on micro accounts **breaks** traditional servicing  
- CID thesis: **CONNECT** pushes marginal post-bind service toward **~$0** while commission **compounds on renewal**
