# Commercial Insurance Direct — Company Overview

## Purpose

Commercial Insurance Direct, Inc. (CID) is a digital distribution and service platform for small-commercial insurance accounts, focused on businesses generally below $5,000 in annual premium.

CID addresses a structural problem: traditional manual agency workflows can make low-premium commercial accounts uneconomic to acquire and service. CID digitizes the customer journey from trade-specific acquisition through quote, bind, post-bind service, renewal, and relevant product expansion.

CID does not underwrite or take insurance risk. CID distributes carrier products and operates the digital customer and servicing experience.

## What is live

- 10 live vertical insurance URLs serving trade- and profession-specific small-business audiences.
- 8 instant-bind BOP/general-liability segments.
- BOP/GL quote capability in approximately 38 seconds for qualifying risks.
- BOP/GL plus workers' compensation flow completing in under one minute for qualifying risks.
- Workers' compensation live across 10 launch states.
- 27-state licensing footprint, with state availability governed by product, carrier, and licensing status.
- CONNECTQUOTE: customer intake, quote, payment, bind, and CONNECT provisioning workflow.
- CONNECT: post-bind policy vault, certificate access, policy documents, coverage questions, claims initiation, and renewal support.
- Automated ACORD long-form submission rail for non-instant classes and carrier markets not available through an API.

## Business model

CID earns agency commission at bind and renewal. Account value can expand through appropriate multi-line coverage, including workers' compensation and other commercial products. Longer-term optionality includes carrier programs and platform/licensing arrangements.

## Strategic position

CID is designed around Reliable, Scalable, and Sellable:

- Reliable: traceable quote, bind, service, certificate, claims-initiation, renewal, and exception workflows.
- Scalable: shared infrastructure across verticals, states, carriers, and products.
- Sellable: documented architecture, operating processes, integrations, product rails, and decision history that support strategic diligence and transfer.

## Current capital objective

CID is raising a $250,000 SAFE to identify the first repeatable channel × vertical × state acquisition wedge. The product infrastructure is live. The capital funds measured distribution validation, including vertical partners, suppliers/distributors, compliant direct outreach, high-intent search, conversion optimization, and workers' compensation customer acquisition across launch states.

## Intended strategic outcome

CID is being built as a strategic modernization asset for a carrier, PE-backed broker platform, strategic broker, or insurtech buyer that needs a faster way to acquire, bind, service, and retain low-premium commercial accounts.

The company’s internal objective is to create the evidence that makes acquiring CID faster, less expensive, and less risky than rebuilding the capability internally.
