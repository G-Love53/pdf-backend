# Add the CID insurance program to your site or member emails

**For:** your marketing, web, or member-success team  
**Time:** usually same day once we have your name and logo  
**You do not need:** an API key, a developer sprint, or a new vendor contract with a carrier

This is a **link**, not an embed. Your members click, get a quote, and can bind in one sitting. Your logo can appear on the quote page. CID handles insurance, payment, and the policyholder app after bind.

---

## What your member experiences

1. They click **your** button, email, or QR code.
2. They land on a CID quote page for their trade (optionally with your logo at the top).
3. They confirm a short form and see a price.
4. They bind and pay on that page. **You never collect a card.** CID is not the merchant of record — the carrier bills the insured.
5. They get **CID Connect** for COIs, documents, and coverage questions.

You keep the relationship as the referrer. CID is the agency of record.

---

## What we need from you (one email)

Send these to your CID contact:

| Item | Required | Example |
|------|----------|---------|
| Organization name (as it should appear) | Yes | Rocky Mountain PHCC |
| Trade you serve | Yes | Plumbing, electrical, beauty, cleaning, pet, fitness, HVAC, painting |
| First state | Yes | Colorado (we turn on additional states when licensed) |
| Logo | Recommended | PNG or SVG, transparent background, at least 200px wide |
| Where the link will live | Helpful | Website button, member newsletter, portal, QR at an event |
| Contact for the program | Yes | Name + email |

Optional later: a member list (business name, email, city, ZIP). We can issue **prefilled** links so they skip retyping.

That is the whole intake. No API work on your side.

---

## What we send back

Within a business day (often faster):

1. **Your program URL** — unique to you so we can credit every visit and bind.
2. **Suggested button copy** (you may rewrite).
3. **A test checklist** (below).

Treat the URL as yours. You may wrap it in your own short link (`yoursite.org/insurance`) as long as the destination stays the URL we issued. Do not strip the query string — that is how we attribute your program.

## Your steps to go live

### 1. Confirm the test link

Open the URL we sent on your phone and on a desktop.

- [ ] Page loads
- [ ] Your name / logo shows (if you sent a logo)
- [ ] You can move through the form (stop before paying unless we gave you a test path)

If the logo is missing or the trade looks wrong, reply on the same thread. Do not publish yet.

### 2. Put the link in one place first

Pick **one** surface for week one:

- A button on your member or resources page, or
- One line in your next email, or
- A QR at an event / counter

**Suggested button:** `Get a member insurance quote`  
**Suggested email line:** `Members can quote and bind business insurance in minutes — start here:`

Do not run paid ads to this URL without telling us. Attribution still works, but we want to know it is you.

### 3. Do not change the URL

Keep the full link, including everything after `?`.

- Fine: your shortener or redirect → our full URL
- Not fine: copying only the domain, or rebuilding the page and guessing parameters
- Not fine: asking members to “search for us” instead of clicking

### 4. After the first week

Reply with anything your members asked. We send you a simple report:

Visits → quote starts → quotes shown → binds → (Workers’ Comp if they added it)

No member card data is in that report.

## What you do **not** do

- Do not collect premiums or card numbers
- Do not give advice on coverages — send them to the quote page or CID
- Do not paste the link into a PDF that gets scanned and broken; use a button or QR
- Do not share someone else’s program URL (each partner has their own)

## Optional: prefilled member links

If you already email members, send CID a spreadsheet:

`business_name, first_name, last_name, email, city, state, zip`

We return a column of unique links. Their form opens with those fields filled. They still confirm before a quote.

## Who to call when something breaks

| Issue | Who |
|-------|-----|
| Link 404, logo missing, wrong trade | Your CID contact (same thread you used to launch) |
| Member stuck on quote or payment | CID — have them use the email on the quote page |
| Claim or COI after they bought | The member uses CID Connect; CID services the policy |

You are not expected to troubleshoot insurance forms.
